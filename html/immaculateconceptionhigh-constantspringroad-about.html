About Us

Immmaculate Conception High School (ICHS) is a Roman Catholic secondary school for girls in Kingston, Jamaica, located at 152 Constant Spring Road.

History

  • Founding: Scottish Franciscan Sisters established the school in Kingston in 1858 as a boarding and day school for girls.

  • Leadership Transition: The Franciscan Sisters of Allegany took over the school in 1879.

  • Fire & Relocation: After a 1937 fire destroyed the Duke Street campus, the school moved temporarily to Old Hope Road. In 1941, it relocated to the former Constant Spring Hotel property.

  • Growth: Over the decasdes, the campus expanded with new facilities, including a Performing Arts Centre and the Sixth Form spaces, while transitioning away from boarding.

Achievements

  • Academic Excellence: ICHS prepares students for CSEC and CAPE examinations and has topped high school academic rankings, such as the Educate Jamaica rankings.

  • STEM & Innovation: Students have earned regional recognition in technology and science competitions, including first place at the Crea+Her Girls in ICT Hackathon for the "Auto Farmer" agricultural app and medals in Caribbean coding and math olympiads.

  • Sports, Arts & Service: Students compete in swimming, track and field, cultural arts, and community service initiatives across Jamaica.
